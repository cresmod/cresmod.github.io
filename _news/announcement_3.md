Our team member, Zhengrui Huang, ​​published an academic article "A robust multitask deep learning algorithm for Antarctic ice shelf fracture detection from multisource satellite imagery" in Remote Sensing of Environment. This study developed a multitask deep learning algorithm to extract fracture areas and lines. The algorithm was applied to the Thwaites, Amery, and Brunt ice shelves in Antarctica. It also calculated gridded fracture indices from the extracted fracture lines and areas. The proposed algorithm and fracture indices provide a flexible and generalizable fracture mapping framework applicable to optical and SAR data.
